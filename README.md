# Excel Data Analysis Project | Data Technician Bootcamp

## Overview
This project was completed as part of a **Data Technician Bootcamp** and showcases practical spreadsheet and data analysis skills using **Microsoft Excel**. The exercises focused on analysing retail and sales datasets, applying formulas, transforming data, creating summaries, and visualising insights through charts and PivotTables. The workbook included real-world business scenarios involving sales performance, product analysis, customer demographics, and reporting. 【1-03f125】

## Skills Demonstrated

### Excel Functions & Formulas
Applied a range of Excel formulas to calculate, summarise, and analyse data, including:

- `SUM()` for calculating total sales and commission values
- `SUMIF()` for conditional calculations and category-based analysis
- `AVERAGE()` for calculating summary statistics
- `AVERAGEIF()` for conditional averages
- `DATE()` for creating and managing date values
- `MONTH()` and `YEAR()` for extracting time-based information
- `UNIQUE()` for identifying distinct values within datasets
- `VLOOKUP()` for retrieving information from related data tables
- `SWITCH()` for categorising data based on business rules and thresholds

### Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Organised and structured datasets for analysis
- Applied filters to isolate specific records and trends
- Sorted data using multiple criteria
- Used conditional formatting to highlight key insights and patterns
- Improved data readability and usability for reporting purposes

### PivotTables & Reporting
- Created PivotTables to summarise large datasets
- Analysed sales volumes by county, product, customer demographics, and region
- Generated business insights from aggregated data
- Used slicers for interactive filtering and exploration of data
- Developed summary reports to support decision-making processes 【1-03f125】

### Data Visualisation
Created charts and visual reports to communicate findings effectively, including:

- Sales performance charts
- Product comparison visualisations
- Pivot Charts linked to PivotTable reports
- Interactive dashboards using slicers and filters

## Key Analysis Activities

### Retail Sales Dataset
- Filtered and sorted customer data
- Calculated total and average commission values
- Analysed purchasing behaviour by demographic categories
- Used `SUMIF()` to compare product sales across customer groups 【1-03f125】

### Sales Volume Classification
- Built PivotTables to summarise sales by county and product
- Applied the `SWITCH()` function to classify products as:
  - High Sales
  - Medium Sales
  - Low Sales
- Produced structured reports to identify top-performing areas and products 【1-03f125】

### Business Intelligence & Visualisation
- Explored sales trends through PivotTables and charts
- Identified high-performing products and customer segments
- Presented findings using clear and interactive Excel visualisations 【1-03f125】
